Freelancer Modern Patch v1.0 - selected legacy fixes

This is a focused single-player selection, not the entire HD Edition overhaul.
Fixes come from the included official update, JFLP, FLSharp 0.9.2 and the credited
binary edits. Component versions and hashes are recorded in sources.json.

Previously enabled FLSharp fixes
- Expanded/corrected resolution handling and base-information menu spacing.
- Exit deadlocks and quit-message handling.
- Malformed-save loading, rare alchemy crash and missing-DLL crash guards.
- Dealer menus failing to open on repeated clicks, and dealer-menu crashes.
- Windowed cursor visibility, mouse warping and initial mouse position.
- Camera-switch stutter and frame-rate-dependent third-person camera movement.

Additional single-player fixes enabled for v1.0
- waypoint_fixes: keep waypoints from clearing at matching coordinates in another
  system; prevent targeting the player's own ship as a nav-map waypoint.
- waypoint_name_fixes: correct waypoint labels, numbering and mission infocards.
- trade_lane_lights_fix: restore ring lights after a disrupted lane recovers.
- flight_controls_fix: synchronize auto-level/rotation-lock state when a ship is
  initialized after launching or loading, preserving the intended control state.
- blank_faction_fix: show None for factionless ships instead of an empty label.
- shield_capacity_fix: display usable shield capacity after the offline threshold;
  this changes the information shown, not the shield's actual strength.
- pilot_names_fix: avoid truncating long NPC names in information/communication text.
- maneuver_frame_fix: initialize the maneuver HUD before showing it after undocking.
- equip_stat_flicker_fix: avoid transient equipment-information flicker when moving
  equipment or buying the last available copy of an item.
- sub_target_fix: prevent sub-target hotkeys from corrupting the Contact List view.
- formation_list_fix: prevent Formation List hotkey display errors outside formation.

Other included corrections
- JFLP widescreen field of view, fonts, cinematic framing and LOD-transition fixes.
- Checked executable timing-delay and separate cinematic frame-limit corrections.
- Custom borderless scaling with aspect preservation and mouse-coordinate mapping.

The eleven additional FLSharp features were reviewed in the pinned source and
selected independently. Their hook locations do not overlap the custom FPS-menu
or borderless-scaling hooks. No new DLL build or HD assets are needed.
Network/multiplayer options, ammo/balance changes, extended lighting limits and
optional feature restoration remain disabled. Rare bug triggers are not all
independently reproduced; see TestResults.txt for actual validation coverage.

Validation of the additional fixes
- Verified active hook targets inside the loaded FLSharp module for all eleven
  features using read-only process inspection.
- Tester confirmed normal equipment/inventory menus after the requested equipment
  check, followed by launching, nav-map/waypoint use and brief flight.
- HUD, waypoint labels and flight controls were reported normal after the restart.
- No claim is made that every rare original bug trigger was reproduced separately.
