Freelancer Modern Patch v1.0 - validation summary

Scope: single-player, English retail baseline, Windows 11. Primary display:
2560x1440. Default cap 120 FPS; optional 60 and experimental 144 FPS settings.
Original installation was preserved. No global OS/driver/security changes.

Passed checks
- Launch, new game, normal exit, relaunch, docked save and reload.
- Borderless display, physical mouse targeting and Alt+Tab recovery.
- In-game FPS selector: 60/120/144 apply live, hide on other Options pages,
  return with the selected value, and persist across restart.
- Practical 60/120 comparison: normal movement, turning, firing and dialogue,
  with smoother motion at 120. These were consecutive segments, not a replay
  with identical inputs or independent simulation-cadence measurements.
- Optional 144 FPS: flight, extended combat, dock/save/reload, conversations,
  nav map, turret/cockpit views and complete trade-lane entry/travel/exit.
  Voices, lips and gestures were reported synchronized. Extended combat,
  trade-lane and camera checks are user observations, not separate captures.
- Borderless scaling: 1920x1080 fills the 2560x1440 display; mouse clicks align
  on the resolution control and FPS arrows. 1600x1200 yields centered 4:3 output
  with black bars and aligned menu clicks. Alt+Tab and return to 1440p passed.
- Scaled 1080p flight: normal user-reported steering/aiming and HUD behavior;
  visual observation confirmed ship/HUD with normal proportions and no clipping.

The 144 FPS conversation capture includes one 803.61 ms gap. The tester switched
away during this run and reported normal focus-loss pausing and recovery, but
foreground state was not logged, so exact attribution is not proven. Excluding
only that interval would give 143.94 FPS; the table retains the unfiltered mean.
The tester may also have been unfocused at the beginning of the scaled-flight
capture. All 4314 positive intervals were retained; none exceeded 16.67 ms.
Presentation measurements are not simulation correctness or input-latency tests.

Automated checks
- Baseline and payload SHA256 validation; 29 payload files in this release.
- Launcher: round-trip all display modes; reject unrelated EXE corruption,
  truncated EXEs, unknown mode bytes, invalid mode names and the retail EXE.
- Scaling: aspect fit, centered bars, coordinate bounds and inverse mapping.
- Custom native components built for x86 with warnings treated as errors.
- Release archive integrity and included source/component hashes checked.

Known limits
- 144 FPS retains its EXP. label; this is not a full-campaign certification.
- Other languages, modded installs, multiplayer, ultrawide monitors and legacy
  external saves are untested. No multiplayer compatibility claim is made.
- FPS changes save immediately; stock Default/Cancel do not undo the custom cap.
- The game normally pauses while unfocused. Background-running was not enabled.
- Scaled lower-resolution textures/text may be softer than native resolution.
- Alt+Enter's exclusive-fullscreen bypass exists but was not manually tested
  after adding scaling. No exhaustive multi-monitor certification is claimed.

Additional legacy fixes enabled for v1.0
Eleven independent FLSharp 0.9.2 single-player fixes were enabled after reviewing
their pinned source and hook locations. See BugFixes.txt for the complete list.
This is a configuration-only change: no new component binary or game asset.
Rare bug triggers are not all independently reproduced. Focused smoke-test
results are recorded in BugFixes.txt; multiplayer options remain disabled.
